# Fiber-Optic Pricing and Promotions

> **Synthetic document.** Vantrix Communications is a fictional telecom

Policy ID: **POL-FIB-022**. Current pricing and active promotions for
Vantrix Fiber, effective the date in `last_updated` above. Fiber pricing
is revisited quarterly; this document is the source of truth, not any
cached marketing page.

## Fiber Speed Tiers

| Tier | Download | Upload | Month-to-Month | 1-Year | 2-Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fiber 300 | 300 Mbps | 300 Mbps | $59.99 | $49.99 | $44.99 |
| Fiber 500 | 500 Mbps | 500 Mbps | $74.99 | $64.99 | $59.99 |
| Fiber 1 Gig | 1000 Mbps | 1000 Mbps | $94.99 | $84.99 | $79.99 |
| Fiber 2 Gig | 2000 Mbps | 2000 Mbps | $134.99 | $124.99 | $119.99 |

All tiers are symmetric (upload = download), which Vantrix markets as a
differentiator against cable competitors' asymmetric plans. Prices
include a Vantrix-supplied ONT and Wi-Fi 6 router at no extra rental
fee — a change from the pre-2025 pricing structure, which charged a
separate $12/month equipment rental.

## New-Customer Promotion: FIBER200OFF

New fiber customers signing a 1-year or 2-year contract receive **$200
off**, applied as a statement credit of **$25/month for the first 8
months**. Promo code **FIBER200OFF**, valid through the date on this
document's `last_updated` quarter; check the current marketing site for
the live expiration date before quoting it to a customer, since this
document is refreshed quarterly but promotions can end mid-quarter.
Month-to-month signups are not eligible for FIBER200OFF; the promotion
is specifically designed to incentivize term contracts.

## Bundle Discount

Customers who add Vantrix Home Phone or a streaming TV package to a
fiber plan receive a **10% bundle discount** on the total monthly bill,
stacking with FIBER200OFF during the promotional window. The bundle
discount does not require a term contract and applies to month-to-month
accounts as well.

## Fiber Availability and the DSL/Cable Churn Gap

Fiber is currently available in roughly 60% of Vantrix's service
footprint; the remainder is served by DSL or cable. Internally, fiber
customers have shown a **higher** churn rate than DSL customers in
historical data (42% vs 19%), which the product team attributes to
fiber's rollout being concentrated in competitive urban markets where
rival providers also offer fiber, rather than any quality issue with the
service itself — see the project glossary entry on this finding for the
modeling-side discussion of why this is treated as a market-competition
signal rather than a product-quality one.

## Price-Lock Guarantee

A 2-year fiber contract locks the quoted monthly rate for the full
24 months even if Vantrix raises list prices during that window — this
is the main differentiator promoted against month-to-month pricing,
which is subject to standard annual rate adjustments (typically
announced 30 days in advance via email).

## Senior and Military Discounts

Vantrix offers a standing **15% discount** off any fiber tier for
customers 65+ or active-duty/veteran military, stackable with the bundle
discount but not with FIBER200OFF (customers get whichever discount is
larger, not both). Verification is a one-time ID check at signup, no
renewal required.
