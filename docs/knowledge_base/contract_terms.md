# Contract Terms and Early Termination Fees

> **Synthetic document.** Vantrix Communications is a fictional telecom
> company invented for this project. All policy IDs, dollar amounts, and
> procedures below are made up for retrieval-testing purposes and do not
> describe any real company's terms.

Policy ID: **POL-CTR-009**. Vantrix offers three contract types at
signup. This document defines each one and the early termination fee
(ETF) that applies if a customer leaves before the term ends.

## Month-to-Month

No fixed term, no ETF. Billing renews automatically every 30 days.
Pricing is **$15-20/month higher** than the equivalent 1-year or 2-year
plan for the same service tier, since Vantrix prices in the flexibility.
A customer can cancel at any time with no penalty beyond the current
paid cycle (see Refund and Cancellation Policy, POL-REF-014, for
proration rules). Month-to-month is the default plan assigned to any
customer who does not actively select a term contract at signup.

## 1-Year Contract

A 12-month commitment at a discounted monthly rate. The early
termination fee is **$15 per month remaining** on the contract at the
time of cancellation, capped at **$180 total**. Example: a customer who
cancels with 8 months remaining owes 8 × $15 = $120. The 1-year plan
includes one free router upgrade at the 6-month mark and waives the
standard $49.99 installation fee.

## 2-Year Contract

A 24-month commitment at the lowest available monthly rate — typically
**$25-30/month cheaper** than month-to-month for the same tier. The
early termination fee is **$20 per month remaining**, capped at **$300
total**. The 2-year plan includes free installation, a free router
upgrade at months 6 and 18, and locks in the promotional rate for the
full term even if list prices increase (see Fiber-Optic Pricing and
Promotions, POL-FIB-022, for current promotional rates).

## ETF Waiver Conditions

The early termination fee is waived in full under any of these
conditions, regardless of contract type:
- The customer is relocating to an address where Vantrix does not offer
  service (proof of new address required — a utility bill or lease).
- Vantrix fails to meet the Support SLA's uptime guarantee (POL-SLA-007)
  three or more times in a rolling 90-day window.
- Active-duty military relocation under PCS orders (standard industry
  accommodation, requires orders documentation).
- Death of the account holder (fee waived for the estate; no
  documentation burden placed on grieving family members beyond a death
  certificate).

## Contract Renewal and Auto-Renewal

1-year and 2-year contracts **do not** silently auto-renew into another
full term. At 45 days before contract end, Vantrix sends a renewal
notice; if the customer takes no action, the account rolls onto
month-to-month pricing at the end of the term, not into a new locked
contract. A customer wishing to re-lock a discounted rate must
explicitly opt in to a new term.

## Mid-Contract Upgrades and Downgrades

Upgrading speed tier mid-contract never triggers an ETF or resets the
contract clock — the existing end date and remaining-months calculation
carry over unchanged. Downgrading speed tier mid-contract is treated as
a partial plan change, not a cancellation, and also does not trigger an
ETF, but may forfeit term-specific promotional pricing tied to the
original higher tier.
